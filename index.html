How to play

⌨️

Desktop

  • AD / ←→ Move
  • SPACE / W Jump · double jump
  • hold SPACE Helicopter glide
  • X / J Punch
  • P / ESC Pause
  • M Mute
📱

Mobile

  • Drag the left stick to run
  • Tap JUMP twice to double jump
  • Hold JUMP while falling to glide
  • Tap PUNCH to slap enemies
  • Pause with the button up top
